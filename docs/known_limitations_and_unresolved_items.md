## 1. Documented Scientific & Methodological Limitations

1. **Ascertainment Bias on Indicine Alleles:**
   - The Illumina BovineHD BeadChip was optimized using *Bos taurus* taurine reference animals. While high marker density ($>777\text{k}$) minimizes this distortion for runs of homozygosity detection, rare indicine-specific private variants are inevitably under-represented compared to deep whole-genome sequencing (WGS).
2. **Phenotypic Data Absence:**
   - The GRRFAC dataset contains high-quality geographic, breed, and country-of-origin metadata, but lacks individual animal-level production phenotypes (e.g. daily milk yield, carcass weight, somatic cell count) or quantified parasite challenge titers (e.g. PCV under trypanosome infection).
   - Consequently, functional candidate genes in autozygosity islands (e.g., *TICAM1*, *HSP90AA1*) represent **evolutionary hypotheses** supported by prior experimental literature, not direct genome-wide association study (GWAS) trait associations in this specific cohort.
3. **Unequal Breed Sample Sizes:**
   - Sample sizes range from $N=140$ (N'Dama) to $N=10$ (localized Ethiopian landraces). While lineage-level groupings have immense power ($N \ge 173$), individual breed inbreeding rankings for cohorts with $N < 15$ carry wider confidence intervals.
4. **Absence of Functional In Vitro / In Vivo Validation:**
   - Candidate regulatory variants within identified autozygosity islands have not been functionally validated via luciferase assays or CRISPR editing in bovine cell lines.
